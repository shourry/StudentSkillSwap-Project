The Campus Dilemma

Why Student SkillSwap?

College students often lack practical peer-sharing avenues. SkillSwap brings a structured, respectful campus marketplace.

⚠️ 1. Classroom Dependence
The Problem

Students depend only on theoretical classroom syllabus or expensive third-party online courses.

✨ SkillSwap Solution:

Free, hands-on peer-to-peer learning with fellow BIST seniors and batchmates.

🤐 2. Hesitation to Ask
The Problem

Students hesitate or feel awkward to directly message classmates asking for tutoring or help.

✨ SkillSwap Solution:

A formal learning request system removes awkwardness and establishes clear learning goals.

📱 3. Chaotic Group Chats
The Problem

WhatsApp and Telegram groups get drowned in spam, making knowledge sharing noisy and unorganized.

✨ SkillSwap Solution:

Structured skill profiles, verified ratings, 1-on-1 scheduled sessions, and private messaging.

3 Simple Steps

How SkillSwap Works at BIST

From discovering a mentor to completing a hands-on learning session.

1
Build Your Skill Profile

Sign up with your BIST email & enrollment number. Add skills you can teach (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) and skills you want to learn.

2
Smart Match & Request

Search by skill, department, or rating. Send a learning request with your preferred mode (Online or Campus Library offline).

3
Session & Peer Feedback

Coordinate via in-app chat, schedule a convenient session slot, learn together, and rate each other to build campus credibility.
